# First Rain — Ideal Client Profile
# Load when: scoring leads, outbound prospecting, evaluating new enquiries.

## Who we target
- Revenue: ₹100 Crore+ annual turnover
- Exhibit frequency: 2+ shows per year
- Geography priority India (in order): Mumbai · Bengaluru · New Delhi · Greater Noida · Hyderabad · Chennai
- Geography priority International (in order): Europe · South East Asia · China · Middle East · UK (new market)
- Decision-maker titles: Head of Marketing / VP Marketing / Exhibition Manager /
  Brand Manager / Corporate Communications Director
- Core fear: Exhibition failure = brand damage + personal career risk

## Priority sectors
Smart Metering/Energy · HVAC and Building Automation · Pharma/APIs/Devices
Architecture and Building Materials · Industrial Manufacturing · Specialty Chemicals
AV and Tech · Nutraceuticals · Beauty/Fragrance · Rail · Textile Machinery
Construction Machinery · Stones

## Disqualify immediately if
- Event agency or interior designer
- Competitor fabricator
- Stall enquiry under 9 sqm
- No brand recognisability
- Cannot confirm show date or budget range

## ICP scoring (use for every new lead)
- Company revenue: ₹100Cr+ = 3pts | ₹50-100Cr = 2pts | Below = 1pt
- Show frequency: 3+/yr = 3pts | 2/yr = 2pts | 1/yr = 1pt
- Decision maker access: Direct contact = 3pts | LinkedIn visible = 2pts | Unknown = 1pt
- Sector fit: Priority sector = 3pts | Adjacent = 2pts | Other = 1pt
Total 10-12 = Tier 1 | 7-9 = Tier 2 | Below 7 = Tier 3 or Discard

## Qualification framework: BBANTI
- B: Brief quality — specific enough to quote?
- B: Budget — range indicated or inferable?
- A: Authority — is the contact the decision-maker?
- N: Need — genuine exhibition need confirmed?
- T: Timeline — show date confirmed and achievable?
- I: Involvement — how many people in the decision?
