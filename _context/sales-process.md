## Offer: what First Rain sells
Custom exhibition stand design and build. Always custom. Never modular.
- India domestic shows: typical ₹8–15L per project
- International shows: typical ₹35–42L per project (based on Secure Meters benchmark)
- Service: concept → design → fabrication → logistics → installation → dismantling
- International fabrication via partners: GH Display (UK), Saltwater/Ashok (Europe), Jack Zuo (US)

## Qualification framework: BBANTI
Run on every new enquiry before investing design time.
- **B — Brief quality:** Specific enough to quote? Show name, sqm, open sides, objectives?
- **B — Budget:** Range indicated or inferable from company size and show?
- **A — Authority:** Is the contact the actual decision-maker?
- **N — Need:** Genuine exhibition need confirmed? (Not "just exploring")
- **T — Timeline:** Show date confirmed and achievable? (8+ weeks minimum)
- **I — Involvement:** How many people in the decision? More = longer cycle.

All 6 must be at least partially answered before moving to quote stage.

## ICP scoring (run on every lead)
- Company revenue: ₹100Cr+ = 3pts | ₹50-100Cr = 2pts | Below = 1pt
- Show frequency: 3+/yr = 3pts | 2/yr = 2pts | 1/yr = 1pt
- Decision maker access: Direct contact = 3pts | LinkedIn visible = 2pts | Unknown = 1pt
- Sector fit: Priority sector = 3pts | Adjacent = 2pts | Other = 1pt

Total 10-12 = **Tier 1** → Niloy personal outreach, all 4 channels
Total 7-9 = **Tier 2** → Lemlist sequence, Dhruv executes
Total below 7 = **Tier 3** → Nurture only or Discard

## Disqualify immediately if
- Event agency or interior designer
- Competitor fabricator
- Stall enquiry under 9 sqm
- No brand recognisability
- Cannot confirm show date or budget range

## Quote checklist (all 4 must pass before issuing)
1. SP − CP above 33% of SP (India) or 38% (International)?
2. Secure Meters still below 25% of annual revenue after this deal?
3. Client account above ₹15L for this FY?
4. Fabrication cost risk in new geography assessed?
4/4 pass → Proceed | Any fail → Flag and reprice

## 5-point decision standard (run before every major recommendation)
1. Does this protect contribution margin?
2. Does this reduce Secure Meters concentration?
3. Does this reduce single-point dependencies?
4. Does this increase system resilience?
5. Does this strengthen enterprise positioning?
3+ No → flag before proceeding.

## Pipeline stage definitions
1. **Lead** — ICP scored, not yet qualified via BBANTI
2. **Qualified** — BBANTI passed, brief received or requested
3. **Quote Sent** — SP issued, margin gate passed
4. **Negotiation** — Client engaging on scope/price
5. **PO Received** — Purchase order in hand (required for Closed Won)
6. **Closed Won** — PO confirmed, production can begin
7. **Closed Lost** — Document reason. Feed back to competitive-landscape.md.
