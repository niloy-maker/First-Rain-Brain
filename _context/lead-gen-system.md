## The two-engine model
Engine 1 — ABM Outbound: 50-75 named accounts, coordinated multi-touch, high precision
Engine 2 — Inbound Pull: Show-specific SEO + Google Ads, higher volume, filter at ICP

## SLA — non-negotiable
Exhibitor list → Campaign live: 72 hours maximum. No exceptions.

## Engine 1: 4-Touch Outbound Sequence (per account, show-specific)
| Touch | Timing | Action |
|-------|--------|--------|
| LI Warm-Up | Week 1, Day 1 | Follow company. No-note connection request. |
| LI Engage | Week 1, Day 3-5 | Like or comment one relevant post. Genuine only. |
| Email Touch 1 | Week 2 | Show reference + fear hook + sector case study. CTA: case study. |
| LinkedIn DM | Week 3 | Short. Human. References the email. |
| Email Touch 2 | Week 4 | Value asset (checklist, show insight). CTA: 20-min brief review. |
| Call | Week 6 | Dhruv for Tier 2 / Niloy for Tier 1. |
| WhatsApp | Week 8 | Only if no response across all prior touches. |

Discard rule: No response across 6 touches in 8 weeks → Tier 3 nurture.

## LinkedIn rules
- NO NOTE on connection request (55-68% acceptance vs 28-45% with note)
- Message 2: ONE sector-matched stand image — never a portfolio dump
- Profile prerequisite: Headline + Featured (3 stand images) + About updated

**Strategic Pivot:** Concentrate 85% of spend on high-ticket cities (Mumbai, Bangalore, Chennai, Hyderabad) and keep Delhi capped at ~15%, compared to current state where Delhi alone consumed 26.6% of spend.

**Projected Result:** 20–23 conversions per month (vs current 15), blended CPA ₹1,300–1,500 (vs current ₹1,612), with materially higher pipeline quality due to concentration in high-value cities.

### 1. Key Findings from Q4 FY25-26 Data

**Keyword Findings**
- Six keywords spent ₹10,300 with zero conversions — pause immediately.
- Long-form "exhibition stall X in [city]" variants consistently outperform short-form "stall fabricators in [city]". The word "exhibition" filters for commercial intent.
- Mumbai: fabricator queries convert at ₹1,104 CPA; designer queries at ₹4,833 CPA (execution-stage vs research-stage intent).
- Bangalore: designer queries convert (₹1,359 CPA); fabricator queries do not (₹2,503 CPA).
- "Booth" and "stand" variants were never tested despite being common search terms.

**Hour-of-Day Findings**
- 10 PM–8 AM: ₹2,600 spent in Q4, zero conversions. Complete waste.
- 7 PM (hour 19): CPA ₹628, CVR 15%. Best hour in the account, receives only 3% of budget.
- 10 AM: click trap — 34 clicks, 1 conversion, CPA ₹4,866.
- Prime zone (11 AM–5 PM): 76% of budget, 85% of conversions.

**Day-of-Week Findings**

| Day | Spend | Conv | CPA | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Friday | ₹13,116 | 11 | ₹1,192 | Best workhorse |
| Sunday | ₹2,544 | 3 | ₹848 | Best CPA, underfunded |
| Saturday | ₹8,900 | 7 | ₹1,271 | Strong |
| Tuesday | ₹14,865 | 10 | ₹1,486 | Solid volume |
| Thursday | ₹10,262 | 6 | ₹1,710 | Mediocre |
| Monday | ₹14,869 | 6 | ₹2,478 | High spend, weak return |
| Wednesday | ₹9,599 | 3 | ₹3,200 | Worst day |

Monday + Wednesday = 33% of budget, 20% of conversions.

**Landing Page Findings**
- 106 URLs in account; only 6 received traffic. 100 dead pages need cleanup.
- Mumbai page + Delhi page convert at ~9.7% CVR — but Delhi converts low-ticket traffic.
- Bangalore, Chennai, Hyderabad pages use Delhi template and convert at only 4.9–6.7%.
- Generic india-exhibits page: worst at 4.6% CVR. Do not use.

**The Delhi Insight (Client-Confirmed)**
- Delhi does not deliver high-ticket clients. Adjusted CPA closer to ₹2,800 per deal.
- Delhi capped at ~15% of budget (was 26.6%). Mumbai page is the model, not Delhi.
