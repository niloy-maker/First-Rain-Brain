## The two-engine model
Engine 1 — ABM Outbound: 50-75 named accounts, coordinated multi-touch, high precision
Engine 2 — Inbound Pull: Show-specific SEO + Google Ads, higher volume, filter at ICP

## SLA — non-negotiable
Exhibitor list → Campaign live: 72 hours maximum. No exceptions.

## Engine 1: 4-Touch Outbound Sequence (per account, show-specific)
| Touch | Timing | Action |
|-------|--------|--------|
| LI Warm-Up | Week 1, Day 1 | Follow company. No-note connection request. |
| LI Engage | Week 1, Day 3-5 | Like or comment one relevant post. Genuine only. |
| Email Touch 1 | Week 2 | Show reference + fear hook + sector case study. CTA: case study. |
| LinkedIn DM | Week 3 | Short. Human. References the email. |
| Email Touch 2 | Week 4 | Value asset (checklist, show insight). CTA: 20-min brief review. |
| Call | Week 6 | Dhruv for Tier 2 / Niloy for Tier 1. |
| WhatsApp | Week 8 | Only if no response across all prior touches. |

Discard rule: No response across 6 touches in 8 weeks → Tier 3 nurture.

## LinkedIn rules
- NO NOTE on connection request (55-68% acceptance vs 28-45% with note)
- Message 2: ONE sector-matched stand image — never a portfolio dump
- Profile prerequisite: Headline + Featured (3 stand images) + About updated

## Engine 2: Google Ads structure
Campaign 1 — Show-Specific: [Show Name] + stand/stall/booth + design/build
Campaign 2 — Sector-Specific: [Sector] + exhibition + stand design
Campaign 3 — Competitor Conquest: intent searches on competitor names

Negative keywords (add immediately):
cheap, affordable, low cost, budget, modular, portable, DIY, rental,
event management, wedding, college, interior design, home, residential
