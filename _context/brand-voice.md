## Who we're talking to (The Character)
- Marketing CXOs of large international and Indian MNC brands
- Junior staff tasked with shortlisting vendors
- Identity transformation: they arrive **Sceptical** → they leave **Confident**
- What they want: a high-quality, impactful trade show stall
- Trade shows are often their primary source of brand exposure — the stakes are high

## Their problem (always lead here, not with our capabilities)
**External:** A high-quality, impactful trade show stall that projects the right brand image is complex to execute — timely execution, ownership, and quality all have to align.

**Internal:** They feel anxious, uncertain, and sceptical. They fear a poorly executed stall will jeopardise both their brand's image and their own personal credibility.

**Philosophical:** They've already invested considerably in brand-building and trade show fees. They shouldn't have to risk that investment on a vendor who can't deliver.

> Side note: Lead with the positive outcome. Don't dwell on the negative past experience.

## Our empathy statement (use to open cold outreach and proposals)
> "We understand how crucial your brand reputation is — and you simply can't afford to jeopardise it with a poorly executed stall."

> Alternative: "We've seen first-hand how frustrating it is to juggle last-minute stall issues instead of focusing on your real goal — engaging prospects and showcasing your brand. You deserve a partner who truly has your back."

## Success we deliver (use to close proposals and emails)
- Seamless, on-time delivery — no scrambling at opening
- A stand that reflects the brand — instantly engaging
- Confidence and peace of mind — focus on leads, not logistics
- Enhanced professional reputation — looks good to boss and colleagues

## Failure we help them avoid (use in subject lines and outbound hooks)

## How Niloy writes
- Direct. Data first. No filler.
- Short sentences. One idea per paragraph.
- Never "I hope this finds you well"
- To clients: warm but direct, first name, clear issue
- Internally: facts and next steps only
- Client emails: under 150 words | WhatsApp: under 80 words

## Write like this
"Rahul — following up on the BES balance of ₹6.3L.
Need this cleared this week. Please confirm date."

## Never write like this
"Hi Rahul, hope you're doing well! Just wanted to touch base regarding the
outstanding balance and see if there's any update at your earliest convenience."
