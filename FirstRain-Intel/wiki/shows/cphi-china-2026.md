# CPhI & PMEC China 2026

## Summary
CPhI & PMEC China is the premier pharmaceutical ingredients and manufacturing equipment trade show in China. The 2026 edition takes place at SNIEC, Shanghai over three days in June. A significant number of Indian pharma and API companies exhibit here annually, making it a key hunting ground for First Rain's international outreach.

## Key Facts
- **Full Name:** CPhI & PMEC China 2026
- **Dates:** 16–18 June 2026
- **Venue:** SNIEC (Shanghai New International Expo Centre), Shanghai, China
- **Sector:** Pharma (APIs, formulations, excipients, manufacturing equipment)
- **Geography:** China (international show; strong Indian exhibitor presence)
- **Source:** Freckle.io enriched exhibitor data, pulled 8 April 2026
