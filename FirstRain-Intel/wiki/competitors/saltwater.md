# Saltwater Studio (SWD)

## Summary
A Mumbai-based exhibition stand design and build firm with a notably premium, design-forward positioning. Operates as three legal entities: SWD Studio (India), Saltwater Studio F.Z.C. (UAE), and an office in Poland. Distinct from Propshop in that they are clearly pitching quality and design sophistication over volume. Strong Europe presence — "crafting refined hospitality for Europe's premier trade shows" is a headline claim, not a footnote. Relevant overlap with First Rain on specialty chemicals clients (20 Microns, Coventya) and electrical/energy (Bharat Bijlee).

- **Website:** saltwaterin.com
- **HQ:** Mumbai, India
- **Offices / Entities:** India (Mumbai, Delhi, Chennai), UAE (Dubai — F.Z.C. entity), Poland
- **Fabrication Workshops:** Mumbai, Delhi, Chennai, Dubai
- **Geographic Claim:** Asia, Europe, America

## Pricing Signals

**No pricing listed.** Premium positioning implies above-market rates.

The Vilokan Group testimonial ("all promised prices were upheld") is the most useful signal: it suggests their pitch includes clear pricing commitments, and this is something clients remember — possibly because others in the market (including competitors) have a reputation for scope creep or late cost additions.

[inference] Saltwater likely operates at the mid-to-premium price band. Given their Europe/UAE positioning and smaller apparent scale vs. Propshop, they are probably not the cheapest option in any market. They appear to win on design quality and reliability rather than price.

## Notes & Observations

- Saltwater is a more direct competitive threat in the **premium, design-conscious, internationally-active** client segment than Propshop. Propshop wins on volume and price; Saltwater competes on quality and design — closer to First Rain's own positioning.
- The **Vilokan Group / IFAT 2024** case study is notable: a Swedish environmental company chose an Indian stand builder for a major Munich show. This is the kind of client First Rain likely cannot currently pursue without European build infrastructure.
- **DDE UHP** appears in both Propshop's client list and Saltwater's testimonials — suggesting DDE multi-sources or has switched. Not a First Rain client, but worth noting as an account that shops around.
- [inference] The Poland office was likely opened to serve European clients more credibly. If First Rain's international work grows (Bangkok, Birmingham are already in their calendar for Secure Meters), a similar infrastructure question will arise.
- The named contact at Ion Exchange — **Hutoxi Batliwala (VP Corporate Comms)** — is a senior marketing decision maker at a large chemicals company. Ion Exchange could be a strong prospect for First Rain; this signals Saltwater already has a relationship there.
