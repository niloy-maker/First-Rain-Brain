# The Prop Shop India (Propshop)

## Summary
A Mumbai-based exhibition stand design and build firm positioning itself as India's No.1. Broad generalist coverage across 20+ sectors, active internationally, heavy marketing spend (Google CPC). 13 years in business, claims 500 clients and 1,000 stands delivered. Direct competitor on mid-to-large domestic and international projects. **Has worked with Secure Meters — our most critical existing client.**

- **Website:** thepropshopindia.com
- **HQ:** Mumbai, India
- **Founded:** ~2013 (13 years claimed as of 2026)
- **Team size:** ~100 (claimed)
- **Scale claimed:** 500 clients, 1,000 stands, 50+ countries

## Notes & Observations

- Propshop's Google CPC ad (visible in URL) is targeting the keyword "exhibition stand fabrication" — they are buying inbound leads aggressively. First Rain's defence is relationship depth, not search visibility.
- The Scania testimonial (European auto brand) and Starlinger (Austrian packaging machinery) suggest they actively pitch to international brands exhibiting in India — a slightly different ICP to First Rain's India-HQ focus.
- No pharma clients are visible despite pharma being listed as a target sector. This leaves the CPhI India circuit more open.
- [inference] Propshop's broad client roster and volume model likely means lower per-account loyalty. Clients who value deep sector knowledge and relationship continuity are winnable back.
