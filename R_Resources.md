## SECTION 15 — LEAD GENERATION SYSTEM

### 15.1 — Strategic Frame

**Current state:** ~23 qualified leads/month | **Target:** 150 qualified leads/month by Q2 FY27

The current system is show-reactive, not market-proactive. Every campaign triggers when a show is announced, runs 3–4 weeks, and dies. Between shows, the pipeline dries up. **This is a system problem, not a budget problem.**

**The Two-Engine Model:**

| Engine | Type | Logic |
|---|---|---|
| Engine 1 | ABM Outbound | 50–75 named accounts, coordinated multi-touch. High precision, lower volume. |
| Engine 2 | Inbound Pull | Show-specific SEO + Google Ads. Lower precision, higher volume — filter hard at ICP qualification. |

These are not the same motion. Different owners. Different content. Different metrics.

### 15.2 — Engine 1: ABM Outbound Playbook

**Account Tier Structure:**

| Tier | Size | Approach | Goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tier 1 — Strategic | 10–15 accounts | Known brands, confirmed ICP, verifiable show history. All 4 channels. Niloy personally involved. | Meeting within 60 days |
| Tier 2 — Target | 30–40 accounts | ICP-matched, not yet worked with. Semi-automated Lemlist + LinkedIn. Dhruv executes. | Qualify for a call within 90 days |
| Tier 3 — Nurture | 100+ accounts | ICP-fit, no active show signal. Always-on email nurture. | Top-of-mind when planning begins |

**Primary Prospecting Source: Show Exhibitor Lists**
Companies on an exhibitor list have already committed budget to exhibiting. They are in-market. They are the highest-probability prospect pool available.

**Operational flow:**
1. Pankaj (CrossNibble) pulls exhibitor list from show website 12 weeks before show
2. Freckle.io enriches list with decision-maker contacts
3. Google AI Studio ICP qualifier scores each contact
4. Sorted into: Tier 1 (manual outreach) or Tier 2 (Lemlist sequence)
5. Remainder enters Tier 3 always-on nurture

**SLA: Exhibitor list → Campaign live: 72 hours maximum. No exceptions.**

**Priority source shows for exhibitor lists:**
Hannover Messe India · ACREX · India ITME · Chemtech World Expo · Plastindia · Pharmatech India / CPhI India · ELECRAMA · Intersolar India · Reed Exhibitions India events · Messe Frankfurt India events

### 15.3 — The 4-Touch Outbound Sequence (per account, show-specific)

Rule: Cold email after 3–4 warm LinkedIn touches does not feel cold.

| Touch | Timing | Action |
|---|---|---|
| LI Warm-Up | Week 1, Day 1 | Follow company page. Send no-note connection request. |
| LI Engage | Week 1, Day 3–5 | Like or comment on one relevant post. Genuine comment only. |
| Email Touch 1 | Week 2 | Show reference + fear-based hook + sector case study. CTA: not a meeting — offer case study. |
| LinkedIn DM | Week 3 | Short. Human. References the email. |
| Email Touch 2 | Week 4 | Deliver a value asset (checklist, show insight, brief visual audit). CTA: 20-minute brief review. |
| Call | Week 6 | Dhruv for Tier 2 / Niloy for Tier 1. Goal: open a conversation, not close a deal. |
| WhatsApp | Week 8 | Only if no response across all prior touches. Short, direct, human. |

**Discard rule:** No response across all 6 touches in 8 weeks → move to Tier 3 nurture. Do not chase beyond this point.

**Email send schedule:** Touch 1 on Monday. Touches 2, 3, 4 on Wednesday. Peak open rates: Mon/Wed 7–11am. Under 200 words per email. One CTA per email. No multiple asks. No attachments.

**LinkedIn rules:**
- NO NOTE on connection request — no-note averages 55–68% acceptance vs 28–45% with a note
- Message 2 contains ONE sector-matched stall image — never a portfolio dump
- Profile prerequisite before any campaign: Headline + Featured (3 stall images: Energy, Pharma, International) + About section updated

### 1. Key Findings from Q4 FY25-26 Data

**Keyword Findings**
- Six keywords spent ₹10,300 with zero conversions — pause immediately.

- Mumbai: fabricator queries convert at ₹1,104 CPA; designer queries at ₹4,833 CPA (execution-stage vs research-stage intent).
- Bangalore: designer queries convert (₹1,359 CPA); fabricator queries do not (₹2,503 CPA).
- "Booth" and "stand" variants were never tested despite being common search terms.

**Hour-of-Day Findings**
- 10 PM–8 AM: ₹2,600 spent in Q4, zero conversions. Complete waste.
- 7 PM (hour 19): CPA ₹628, CVR 15%. Best hour in the account, receives only 3% of budget.
- 10 AM: click trap — 34 clicks, 1 conversion, CPA ₹4,866.
- Prime zone (11 AM–5 PM): 76% of budget, 85% of conversions.
