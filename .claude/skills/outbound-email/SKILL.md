## Ask before writing
1. Which show is this sequence for?
2. Which sector / company type?
3. Do we have a case study for this sector?

## 4-Touch sequence structure

### Touch 1 — Email (Monday)
Subject: Fear-based, show-specific, curiosity gap
Format: Show reference + fear hook + sector case study offer
CTA: Not a meeting — offer the case study
Under 200 words. One CTA only.

### Touch 2 — LinkedIn DM (Week 3)
Short. Human. References the email sent.
Under 80 words.

### Touch 3 — Email (Week 4)
Deliver a value asset (checklist, show insight, stand audit).
CTA: 20-minute brief review.
Under 200 words. One CTA only.

### Touch 4 — WhatsApp (Week 8 — only if no response)
Short, direct, human. Under 60 words.

## Rules
- Send Touch 1 on Monday. Touches 2–4 on Wednesday.
- Peak open rates: Mon/Wed 7–11am IST
- No attachments. No multiple asks. One CTA per touch.
- After 4 touches with no response → move to Tier 3 nurture
