# LinkedIn Post — First Rain V2

## Read first
- _context/brand-voice.md

## Ask before writing
1. What is the topic? (show countdown / sector insight / portfolio / lesson)
2. Which show or client to reference? (always specific — never generic)
3. Any numbers or results to include?

## Post structure
Line 1: Hook — specific observation, not a generic statement
Lines 2–4: The story or insight (what happened, what you noticed)
Lines 5–6: The lesson or question
Final line: One question that invites response
Max 3 hashtags at the end

## Rules
- 150–250 words maximum
- Name the show specifically
- Name the challenge specifically
- Never promotional — always observational
- End with a question that invites response

## Good opening lines
- "Most exhibitors at [Show] made the same mistake."
- "20 years of building stands taught me one thing about [sector]."
- "We had 48 hours to fix a [Show] stand. Here's what happened."
