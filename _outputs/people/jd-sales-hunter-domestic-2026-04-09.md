## About First Rain

First Rain Exhibits India Pvt. Ltd. has designed and built custom exhibition stands for 20 years — pharma clients at CPhI, industrial brands at Hannover Messe, consumer goods companies at every major show in India. We don't do modular. Every stand we build is custom: designed to a brief, fabricated to a margin, and measured by the meetings it generates on the show floor. We're growing from ₹6 Cr to ₹24 Cr over three years — and the next phase requires someone who can open doors we haven't knocked on yet.

## The Role

**Sales Hunter — Domestic India**
Location: Mumbai or Delhi (field role — you're at the show floor, not at a desk)
Reporting: Niloy Debnath, Director

You own the top 15 Tier 1 accounts in our domestic ABM pipeline. These are Indian companies — manufacturing, pharma, industrial, FMCG — that exhibit at India's flagship shows (ACREX, Plastivision, IMTEX, CPhI India, Chemtech) with stand budgets of ₹15L–₹50L. Most of them are currently buying from a competitor or running in-house. Your job is to change that.

This is not a farming role. You are not managing existing clients. You are opening new accounts, converting competitor relationships, and building a pipeline from zero. You will be given the ABM list, the outreach tools, and the margin floor. The rest is on you.

## 90-Day Plan

**Day 30** — 15 Tier 1 accounts contacted (personalised, show-specific outreach — not mass email). Every account has a named contact and a first-touch logged.

**Day 60** — Meetings booked with minimum 5 Tier 1 accounts. At least 2 active conversations with a show brief in hand.

**Day 90** — First proposal submitted to a net-new account. Proposal is costed to margin floor (33% CM minimum). No exceptions.

This isn't aspirational. It's the baseline. If Day 30 hasn't happened, we're having a different conversation.

## What We're Looking For

- **3+ years in B2B field sales** — not inside sales, not distributor management. You have personally met buyers, walked shows, and closed deals where the ticket was above ₹10L.
- **Exhibition, events, or MICE experience preferred** — you know how a show floor works. You've walked IMTEX or CPhI or a similar show as a vendor or buyer. You know what a stand brief looks like.
- **Fluent in the Mumbai or Delhi industrial/manufacturing corridor** — you have contacts at marketing heads and procurement leads in at least one of these sectors: pharma, industrial automation, FMCG, chemicals, engineering.
- **Comfortable with long sales cycles (3–9 months)** — exhibition stand decisions aren't impulse buys. You know how to stay in the conversation without being a pest.
- **WhatsApp-first communicator** — our clients respond to WhatsApp before email. You are responsive, brief, and clear. No walls of text.

## Compensation

**Base salary:** ₹6–8L per annum (based on experience)
**Commission:** 4–6% of Selling Price on every project you close

Commission is paid after:
1. Project is delivered to client
2. Contribution margin on the project is confirmed at 33% or above
3. Payment is received in full (Elliott Ebara rule — no verbal, no partial)

**Example:** You close a ₹30L stand for a pharma client at ACREX. Commission at 5% = ₹1.5L, paid on collection. Close 4 projects at this scale in a year = ₹6L commission on top of base.

No cap on commission. No threshold to unlock it (first rupee from the first project). If you protect the margin floor and get paid, you get paid.
