18 sqm island at CPhI Worldwide.

Frankfurt Messe. 300+ booths in three halls. Pharma companies spending €300k on double-deck builds next door.

Our client had 18 square metres.

One decision changed the entire stand.

We suspended a backlit ceiling panel at 2.8m — full footprint, slightly inset. Warm white LED through frosted acrylic. Under it: a custom plinth, 400mm high, backlit from below in cool white. Each piece of packaging placed like it was in a design museum.

No harsh downlight. No fluorescent flicker. No catalogue display.

The ceiling did three things at once:
→ Created a visual boundary — you knew when you were inside the Gerresheimer space
→ Made the light feel warm, not clinical
→ Made transparent packaging glow from two directions

Three days on the floor. 47 qualified meetings. 3 RFQs generated without leaving the booth.

Four weeks later, they briefed us for their USA show. Unprompted.

Budget is not the constraint. Light is.
