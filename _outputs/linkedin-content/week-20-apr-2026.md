Day 1 of install.

There are people on a floor in Mumbai right now — our team. They arrived at 6am. The show opens Thursday.

Most clients never see this part. They see the design presentation, the 3D render, the final walkthrough. The build itself — the 36 hours between an empty carpet and a completed stand — is invisible infrastructure.

It's also where everything either holds or doesn't.

CAD files don't account for the column that's 12cm off-centre. The render doesn't show the cable management problem behind the reception counter. The timeline doesn't include the 11pm call when one panel needs to be remade.

We've been solving these problems on-site for 20 years. Not because we're better at improvising — because the systems we run mean fewer of them happen in the first place. Shop drawings. Pre-fabrication checks. Site supervisor with authority to make decisions.

## THURSDAY, 23 APR — International

---

European shows have a different operating reality. Not harder — different.

Setup windows are tighter. Material certifications are enforced, not suggested. Fire ratings, structural load sign-offs, accessibility compliance — these are checked at the hall, not just on the paperwork.

The brands that struggle at European platforms aren't the ones with weaker products. They're the ones that briefed their stand like it was a Mumbai show.

We've been working internationally for over a decade — Vitafoods Geneva, ACHEMA Frankfurt, CPHI Worldwide Barcelona, among others. The pattern is consistent: international projects that go smoothly started 16 weeks before the show, not eight.
