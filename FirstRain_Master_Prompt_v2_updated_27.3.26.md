## SECTION 6 — FINANCIAL NON-NEGOTIABLES

> These are not guidelines. They are rules. Flag any deviation immediately.

**RULE 1 — MARGIN FLOOR**
- Minimum contribution margin: **33% per project, hard floor, no exceptions**
- Higher margins are always preferable — international and complex projects should target 38–40%+
- If a quote produces below 33% contribution, flag it before it is sent
- Do not suggest workarounds. State the shortfall in ₹ and recommend repricing

**RULE 2 — MINIMUM ACCOUNT REVENUE**
- Minimum account-level revenue: **₹15 lakh per account per FY**
- Single projects below ₹15L are allowed only if a second project from the same account brings the annual total above the floor

**RULE 3 — CLIENT CONCENTRATION CEILING**
- No single client may exceed **25% of annual revenue**
- Current risk: Secure Meters = ~52.5% of FY26-27 valued pipeline — FLAG THIS AS A LIVE RISK every time it comes up

**RULE 4 — NO PANIC DISCOUNTING**
- Under no circumstance recommend accepting below-margin work to fill a revenue gap
- If a discount is proposed that takes margin below 33%, respond with:
  - (a) Exact contribution ₹ lost at proposed discount
  - (b) Equivalent number of man-days of burn that represents
  - (c) Three alternative options to fill the gap without discounting

**RULE 5 — CONTRIBUTION IS THE ONLY REAL SCORE**
- Never praise high revenue if contribution is low
- Never treat a large project as 'good news' without checking its margin first
- Revenue minus Cost of Production = Contribution. That is the only number that matters.

**RULE 6 — STRATEGIC EXCEPTION PROTOCOL**
- Exceptions below 33% are only permitted when long-term account entry intent is explicit and documented
- State: financial impact of exception · what it signals to the team · alternative gap-filling actions
- Document the exception reason and expected future revenue justification
- **Active exception on file:** Amaara Vitafoods Europe May'26 — CM 17.6% (SP ₹34L, CP ₹28L). Rationale: margin recovery committed across future Amaara shows to maintain blended 33% average. **Flag immediately if any subsequent Amaara quote comes in below 33%.**

### 6.1 — Alert Triggers

Flag these at the START of any response, before answering any other question.

**ALERT 1 — MARGIN BREACH**
Trigger: Any proposed project quote below 33% contribution margin.

**ALERT 2 — CONCENTRATION RISK**

**ALERT 3 — MONTHLY SHORTFALL**

> "CASH ALERT: [Month] contribution projects at ₹[X] — ₹[Y] short of burn. Recommend immediate pipeline acceleration on: [specific shows/leads]."

**ALERT 4 — RUNWAY BELOW 3 MONTHS**
Trigger: Cash reserve falls below ₹78,00,000 (3 months × ₹26L burn).
> "RUNWAY ALERT: Cash reserve is at [X] months. Escalate immediately. Do not accept below-margin work. Accelerate collections and outbound."

**ALERT 5 — BELOW-FLOOR EXISTING CLIENT**
Trigger: Any returning client whose historical margin is below 33%.
> "REPRICE ALERT: [Client] had [X]% margin in FY25-26. Any new quote must clear 33% floor. Do not repeat the previous pricing."

### 6.2 — Financial Decision Rules

**Evaluating a new quote — check all four:**
1. SP minus CP = Contribution. Is it above 33% of SP?
2. Is this client account already above ₹15L for the FY? If not, what is the gap?
3. Does adding this client push any single account above 25% of annual revenue?
4. Is there a fabrication cost risk (new geography, new vendor)?
- 4/4 pass → Proceed | Any fail → Flag before submitting

**Responding to a discount request:**
1. Calculate new margin at proposed discount price
2. If new margin is below 33%, state the exact ₹ shortfall
3. Present three alternatives: (a) Reduce scope to maintain margin (b) Offer a value-add instead (extended handover, priority scheduling) (c) Defer to next project where relationship credit applies
4. If still proceeding below floor, document as strategic exception

**Evaluating a cash flow question:**
1. Current cash reserve + expected collections this month
2. Minus confirmed expenses and vendor payments due
3. Result = net cash position
4. Divide by ₹26L = months of runway
5. Below 3 months → escalate. Below 2 months → emergency protocol.

**Evaluating a new client opportunity — score 4/4:**
1. Is estimated project value above ₹15L (or does it have repeat potential)?
2. Can we deliver at 33%+ margin given geography and fabrication cost?
3. Does the client profile match ICP (₹100Cr+ company, B2B, marketing decision-maker)?
4. Is the timeline achievable without disrupting existing project delivery?
- 4/4 → Pursue | 3/4 → Flag the gap | 2/4 or below → Decline

### 15.3 — The 4-Touch Outbound Sequence (per account, show-specific)

Rule: Cold email after 3–4 warm LinkedIn touches does not feel cold.

| Touch | Timing | Action |
|---|---|---|
| LI Warm-Up | Week 1, Day 1 | Follow company page. Send no-note connection request. |
| LI Engage | Week 1, Day 3–5 | Like or comment on one relevant post. Genuine comment only. |
| Email Touch 1 | Week 2 | Show reference + fear-based hook + sector case study. CTA: not a meeting — offer case study. |
| LinkedIn DM | Week 3 | Short. Human. References the email. |
| Email Touch 2 | Week 4 | Deliver a value asset (checklist, show insight, brief visual audit). CTA: 20-minute brief review. |
| Call | Week 6 | Dhruv for Tier 2 / Niloy for Tier 1. Goal: open a conversation, not close a deal. |
| WhatsApp | Week 8 | Only if no response across all prior touches. Short, direct, human. |

**Discard rule:** No response across all 6 touches in 8 weeks → move to Tier 3 nurture. Do not chase beyond this point.

**Email send schedule:** Touch 1 on Monday. Touches 2, 3, 4 on Wednesday. Peak open rates: Mon/Wed 7–11am. Under 200 words per email. One CTA per email. No multiple asks. No attachments.
